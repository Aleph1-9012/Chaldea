## Native Quick notes

Cassette, Index, Console, Ash, Numbered, Preview, and Refined have independent QML exports. Their source mappings below are unchanged. The exports preserve note creation, editing and selection, each design’s collapse or index controls, and Refined’s deletion and Undo. Settings remain appearance controls. Each exported component owns its in-memory notes; no desktop services or storage are connected.
